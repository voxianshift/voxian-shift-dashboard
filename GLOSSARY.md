# Voxian Shift

Career adaptation vocabulary shared across diagnosis, exploration and planning.

## Language

**Exposure**:
The assessed potential for AI to affect occupational tasks. It is not the probability of losing a job.

**Strategy**:
One of four approaches to adaptation: Automate, Hybrid, Pivot or Create.
_Avoid_: Worldline in user-facing prose

**Direction**:
The user's stated preference about staying, growing, changing or exploring work. It can inform a strategy recommendation but does not commit the user.

**Role alternative**:
A possible destination occupation to explore within a strategy. Alternatives are not a promotion sequence.

**Learning phase**:
A chronological portion of an adaptation plan with a focus and deliverable. Phase completion does not imply qualification for a senior role.

**Flight Plan**:
An actionable adaptation plan grounded in a chosen strategy, available time and occupational evidence.

**Commitment**:
The user's confirmed choice to follow a particular plan and schedule.
